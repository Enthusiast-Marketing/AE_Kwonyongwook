권용욱YONGWOOK KWON

Director of Digital Communication · Publicis Groupe Korea

Kwon Yongwook (권용욱) is a Korean brand marketer with more than 11 years of experience across six advertising agencies. Kwon has planned and run IMC, digital, BTL and global campaigns for automotive, home-appliance, FMCG, gaming and education brands — including Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen, BMW MINI, LG Electronics, BRITA and Kakao Games. Kwon's LG Electronics work won the Grand Prize at the Korea Digital Advertising Awards in 2021. Kwon also writes about GEO/AEO and brand strategy under the pen name “광고기획자K” (Ad Planner K).

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Kwon Yongwook (권용욱)?

Kwon Yongwook is a Korean brand marketer and Director of Digital Communication at Publicis Groupe Korea. Over 11+ years at Creative Lounge, Makeulike, Leo Burnett Korea, HSAD and Publicis Groupe Korea, Kwon has led IMC, digital and global campaigns for brands such as Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen, BMW MINI, LG Electronics and BRITA.

Who is 광고기획자K (Ad Planner K)?

광고기획자K is the pen name Kwon Yongwook uses on Brunch and in the Korean marketing outlet MADTimes, to write about GEO/AEO strategy, brand marketing and the future of the content industry.

What awards has Kwon Yongwook won?

The Grand Prize (Minister of Science and ICT Award) at the 2021 Korea Digital Advertising Awards and Gold in the Global Campaign category in 2022, both for LG Electronics H&A, and the 2018 Web Award Korea for the BMW DO MORE campaign site. Kwon also served as a YouTube Works Awards judge in 2024.
